About.

Hi, I'm [your name]

Finance, minus the gatekeeping.

I graduated in May 2026 with an MSc in Finance and Investments from Copenhagen Business School, and started this the same way most of you are reading it: trying to actually understand what was happening in markets each week, not just the headline version.

Wall Street for the Girls is a weekly market story, explained the way I wish someone had explained it to me first: no assumed background, real numbers, and a proper teaching moment underneath every headline. "For the girls" is the point, not a disclaimer. It means nothing here assumes you already have a finance degree, even though, by the end of a few issues, you'll know more than plenty of people who do.

Every issue is built the same way: the story, the concept behind it in the Foundation, the full mechanics in the Financial Syllabus, and real sources in the references. That structure is deliberate. It's the difference between reading market commentary and actually learning markets.

Education

MSc, Finance & Investments, Copenhagen Business School (2026)

What this is

A weekly breakdown of one real market story, plus the Foundation and Financial Syllabus that teach the concepts underneath it.

Why it exists

To learn finance in public, and make markets legible without requiring a finance degree to read the news.

The Free Trial Is Over, and the Subscription Renewed Itself.

The free trial ends, and here is the weird part. Nothing happens. Then one morning you wake up to your bank statement, and there it is.

You might be wondering what the "subscription" actually is. It's the tariffs that were due to expire this week and didn't. Section 301 was written to run four years, then renew indefinitely, with no cap on the rate. The bill didn't lapse. It converted to a plan that keeps going up.
